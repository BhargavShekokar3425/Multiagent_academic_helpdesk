About:

Deeksha Varshney is an Assistant Professor at the Computer Science and Engineering department, Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, Rajasthan, India. She leads the ADHRI-NLP Lab (AI for Development on Human Reasoning and Impact), where her research focuses on Interpretability, Reasoning in Large Language Models, Multilingual LLMs, AI Safety, Trustworthy AI, and AI for Sustainable Impact. She obtained her Ph.D. from the Indian Institute of Technology Patna in 2023. Her doctoral research focused on enhancing dialogue generation models using multi-source heterogeneous information. Prior to joining IIT Jodhpur, she worked as a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (NUS) where she worked on Climate NLP and Sustainable Finance.
